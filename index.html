    Robot calibration

    Collect no-spin shots, fit raw motor input to launch speed, and estimate nozzle height.

    How to collect the data
    1. Use no spin: top and bottom wheel inputs are kept equal.
    2. Measure first-bounce distance in cm. Table mode can reference the net; ground mode always references the back of the robot base.
    3. When calibrating on the table, you can optionally measure clearance from the bottom of the ball to the top of the net.
    4. Press Save value & next. Blank fields are allowed and simply advance the plan.
